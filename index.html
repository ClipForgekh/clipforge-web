Edit one video. Export two hundred.

ClipForge is a desktop video editor for people who post the same kind of clip again and again. Get one video right, save the edit as a recipe, and apply it to a whole folder. Every output is cropped, scored, watermarked, cleaned and renamed.

The editor comes first: a preview and a timeline, like the editors you already know. The batch is one more step at the end, not a separate tool.

A recipe is your edit, written down.

Every change you make is stored as an instruction, not baked into pixels. That's why one edit can be replayed on a different video, and why your source files are never touched.

  • Fits each clip. Crop and framing adapt to each source's size and length.
  • Shareable. Hand a teammate the recipe file and they get the same output.
  • Made for social. Start from presets for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Two hundred videos, not one song two hundred times.

A recipe points at a folder of music, not a single file. Each folder is an audio group, and ClipForge picks a track for every video. The music is looped or trimmed to fit, faded, and lowered while someone speaks.

RandomA different track for each video.

Same for allOne track for the whole batch.

Clean files, checked one by one.

Downloaded videos carry titles, authors, comments and sometimes the GPS position where they were filmed. ClipForge removes them on export, on by default, and keeps only the fields you choose. Then it opens every output and checks.

Built for runs that take all night.

A batch of hundreds can take hours. You should be able to walk away, close the lid, and come back to finished work.

Pause and resume

Stop the queue when you need the computer back. It picks up at the file it stopped on.

Survives a crash

Your project saves after every change. If the app or the computer goes down, finished files stay finished.

Uses your graphics card

Hardware encoding with NVIDIA, Intel, AMD and Apple chips, so long batches finish sooner.
