What you'll need

A 64-bit computer. FFmpeg is included, so there is nothing else to install. A graphics card from NVIDIA, Intel, AMD or Apple makes long batches faster, but isn't required.

ClipForge works offline. Your videos, recipes and sound library stay on your computer.
